## Software used by the pipeline

The pipeline runs these tools; please cite them when you publish results (see the README):
FastQC, MultiQC, fastp, Bowtie2, SAMtools, deepTools, MACS2, FlexPeak, BEDTools, Subread/featureCounts,
DESeq2, ChIPseeker, Homer.
